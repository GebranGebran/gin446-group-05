Service information

Study spaces

Reading rooms and quiet areas where students can work alone or in small groups. A later milestone will list locations and opening hours from real data.

Engineering workshops

Short practical sessions, such as introductory web programming, electronics, and design tools. Registration will be added in a later milestone.

Student support

Academic advising and project mentoring for students who need help planning their courses or their work. Booking will be added in a later milestone.

Career and internship guidance

Help with CVs, internship searches, and interview preparation for engineering students. This service was added to test how the card layout handles a long title.
